💡 Risk Interpretation & Recommendation Prototype

📌 Đây là rule-based prototype, chưa phải production auto-scaling.
🟢 Normal — Score < calibrated threshold (0.05)
Recommendation: Continue monitoring. No action needed.
🟡 Watch — Score near threshold (0.04 – 0.05)
Recommendation: Inspect request/error trend. Prepare for potential spike.
🟠 Warning — Score ≥ calibrated threshold (0.05)
Recommendation: Check traffic spike and backend error rate. Consider scaling.
🔴 Critical — High score + persistent incident phase
Recommendation: Scale service immediately. Investigate backend bottleneck.
